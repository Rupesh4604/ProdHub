ProdHub

ProdHub is a personal productivity web app. It brings your tasks, projects, goals, habits and weekly reviews into one dashboard, so you can plan and track your work without switching between tools.

  • Organise tasks by project, with deadlines, priorities and recurring tasks.
  • Track daily habits and streaks, and set goals broken into projects.
  • Review your week with a summary of completed tasks and habit consistency.
  • Optional Google Calendar sync: with your permission, ProdHub reads the title and start time of your upcoming events (read-only) and shows them next to your task deadlines. It never creates, edits or deletes events.
